Medical Study Companion

A premier study companion designed specifically for post-graduate residency medical students preparing for MD/MS board finals and competitive super-speciality exams (NEET SS). Log study blocks by specialty, track clinical habits, plan key sessions, set spaced repetition recall cards, and maintain peak focus.

🩺 Clinical Analytics Dashboard: Check your duration, frequency, and spacing consistency index for the past week and month, evaluated with chief resident appraisal messages.

🎯 Session Board Targets: Define micro-goals and tasks for each session blocks, marking them completed straight away to maintain clinical flow.

🧠 Spaced Recall Flashcards: Interactive clinical case cards using native database models to guarantee elite super-specialist retention.
